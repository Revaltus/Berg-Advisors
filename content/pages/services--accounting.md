## A small business accountant who treats you like family

Berg Advisors is a virtual CPA firm serving clients across Pennsylvania and nationwide. Our team prioritizes getting to know your business, communicating before you have to chase us down, and charging a flat monthly fee so an invoice never surprises you. Whether you run a multi-channel e-commerce brand, manage a portfolio of rental properties, or oversee a family office with accounts scattered across entities, you get a team that treats your business like part of the family.

## Outsourced accounting and bookkeeping built around your business

Bookkeeping is the foundation everything else sits on, and it's usually the first thing that slips when you're busy running the business. Our outsourced accounting and bookkeeping services cover the monthly close, bank and credit card reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable management, and financial reporting that tells you something you can actually use. Instead of scrambling to reconcile a dozen accounts before tax season, you get clean books delivered every month, with a team available to explain what the numbers mean for your next move.

Every engagement runs on a flat monthly fee, so a busy month never turns into a surprise bill. At the start of the relationship, we build a mutual success plan with you, so the reporting you receive matches how you run your business. Small business owners in Pennsylvania and across the nation hire Berg Advisors to take the books off their plate entirely.

## Business tax preparation and planning

Tax season shouldn't be the only time you hear from your accountant. Berg Advisors handles business tax preparation and pairs it with planning conversations throughout the year, so decisions get made in June instead of scrambled together in March.

- Business tax preparation for LLCs, S-corps, and multi-entity structures
- Year-end 1099 preparation and filing
- Ongoing tax planning and strategy, built into your regular check-ins
- Audit protection and representation if the IRS sends a notice
- Multi-state sales tax compliance for product-based businesses expanding into new states

That last item matters more than most local CPAs let on. As e-commerce and wholesale businesses grow, they cross sales tax nexus thresholds in states they've never set foot in, and the penalties for missing it are real.

## Specialized accounting for e-commerce, property management, and family offices

A generalist CPA can file your return. Fewer can reconcile revenue across five sales channels, track owner distributions across a dozen properties, or consolidate accounts that span personal, business, and investment entities. Berg Advisors is one of a limited number of full-service U.S. firms built specifically around accrual accounting, inventory, and multi-state sales tax nexus for product-based businesses.

### Multi-channel e-commerce
icon: Globe

Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart each report revenue differently, and reconciling them by hand leaves gaps nobody catches until tax time. We manage financial data from every platform in one place, so you get a real view of margins by channel instead of a guess. [See our e-commerce accounting services](/industries/e-commerce)

### Property management companies
icon: Building2

Tracking income, expenses, and owner distributions across multiple properties and entities in spreadsheets is how things fall through the cracks. We build one centralized view of every property's performance, so month-end doesn't mean chasing down five different bank statements. [See our property management accounting services](/industries/property-management)

### Family offices
icon: Users

Multiple entities, multiple accounts, and personal and business expenses that tend to blend together create a visibility problem most bookkeepers aren't equipped to solve. We consolidate reporting across every entity so you can see the full picture without spending your own time reconciling it. [See our family office accounting services](/industries/family-offices)

## Guidance for growth, cash flow, and succession planning

Compliance work keeps you legal. Advisory work is what actually moves the business forward, and it's where a lot of firms stop showing up once the tax return is filed. Berg Advisors builds recurring strategic conversations into every relationship, so planning happens on a schedule rather than in a panic.

### Cash flow management
icon: ChartLine

Knowing what's in the bank today matters less than knowing what's coming in 90 days from now. We help you build forecasts you can actually plan around.

### Budget development and review
icon: Calculator

A budget that never gets revisited isn't a budget, it's a document. We review yours against actuals on a regular cadence and flag where it needs to change.

### Recurring consulting calls
icon: Clock

Quarterly or monthly, depending on what your business needs, these calls are where the real strategy conversations happen, not buried in an email thread.

### Succession planning
icon: Target

Whether you're planning an exit in two years or twenty, the financial groundwork has to start now. We help you get the entity structure and reporting in shape before a buyer or successor ever asks to see it.
