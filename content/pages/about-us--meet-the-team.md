## **Getting to Know the Faces Behind Berg Advisors**

While our accounting expertise, rigorous financial advice, and dedication to tax strategy are key to helping your small business or individual wealth management thrive, we know that successful client relationships are built on more than just numbers. At Berg Advisors, we believe that trust, collaboration, and a little bit of personality go a long way in delivering the best financial solutions.

This section is dedicated to peeling back the professional veneer and introducing you to the people who make up Berg Partners, not just as expert CPAs and consultants, but as individuals. You’ll discover the passions, hobbies, and fun facts that make our advisory team unique, giving you a personal glimpse into the minds dedicated to your financial success and offering reliable accounting services.

## Leadership with over 30 years of combined CPA expertise

More than 30 years of combined CPA experience sits behind the advice clients get from this firm. Andrew Berg founded the practice and has been featured on the Profit First Professionals podcast talking about what it takes to modernize a traditional accounting firm without losing the personal touch that makes it work.

### Andrew C. Berg, CPA
Founder

Andrew focuses on outsourced CFO work for inventory-heavy businesses, accrual conversions for companies preparing to bring on a bank or investor, and multi-state sales tax nexus questions for product-based businesses expanding into new territory.

### Joshua Beaugrand, CPA

Joshua brings CPA-level rigor to business and personal tax planning, preparation, and filing, working across entity types and multiple states.

### Lindsey Coburn, CPA

Lindsey manages the monthly close and financial reporting process that turns a set of books into something owners actually use to make decisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Meet Our Expert Team

**Q: Who are the CPAs at Berg Advisors?**
A: The team includes Andrew C. Berg, CPA (founder), Joshua Beaugrand, CPA, Lindsey Coburn, CPA, and Veil Velarde, CPA, alongside Michael Laudazio and Julia Lindner, EA, who handles IRS representation. Together they bring over 30 years of combined CPA experience.

**Q: Does Berg Advisors only serve clients in Pennsylvania?**
A: No. The firm started in Pennsylvania, but operates as a virtual practice serving e-commerce sellers, property management companies, and family offices across the United States, with no geographic limit on who it can help.

**Q: What makes Berg Advisors different from other local CPA firms?**
A: Flat monthly fees instead of hourly billing, a written mutual success plan for every client, and specialist teams for e-commerce, property management, and family offices, rather than one generalist bookkeeping process applied to every client.
