## LinkedIn

You have $86,000 in the bank. A $40,000 production deposit is due in 10 days and a $35,000 balance lands in 24. That leaves $11,000 for card bills and ad spend, and nothing in Seller Central, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, or Walmart Seller Center will tell you so.

That gap is why our CPA team built the Monday Five, a ten-minute weekly check on five numbers: cash against supplier payments, weeks of inventory per top SKU, blended gross margin after fees and returns, inventory value against the balance sheet, and pending marketplace payouts. Each one comes with a plain-English picture of "good" and a trigger for when to call us. Example: a top SKU with 15 weeks on hand and a 10-week lead time has a 5-week window before a stockout. Fine, but not for long.

The catch is that it only works when every number comes from one reconciled set of books. If your Monday sheet takes an hour, the books are not unified yet.
